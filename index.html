Reach
The cowboy guards the castle. His towel only reaches crabs inside the dashed ring; a whip reaches farther.
Prizes · Block
Win a prize each round. Before the next round, drag it from beside the castle onto the sand to hold crabs up for a moment. Prizes stay where you put them until you move them.
Golden towel
Land three different tricks in a row for six seconds of longer, wider, stronger swipes.
Pop-up crabs
They duck under swipes. Tap each one before it runs.
Tap · Snap
A quick snap at one nearby crab.
Swipe · Sweep
Draw a line through several crabs.
Swing & release · Whip
Drag back to wind up, then swing forward and let go. A longer backswing reaches farther.
Circle · Spin
Draw a loop to clear a group.
Hold & release · Power snap
Hold still until the ring fills, then lift.
Purple shell crab

Crowned king crab
Only a spin or power snap clears it. Other tricks knock it back.
