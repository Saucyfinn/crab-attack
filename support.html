About the game

Crab Attack is a beach-themed arcade defense game. Protect your sandcastle from approaching crabs.

Frequently asked questions

The game is not responding. What can I do?

Close and reopen the app. If the issue persists, restart your device and check whether an update is available.

I bought the game. How do I get it on a new device?

Open the App Store with the same Apple Account you bought it with, search for Crab Attack and tap the download button. There's no charge to download it again, and every round is included.

How do I report a bug?

Email support with the steps to reproduce the problem and, if possible, a screenshot.
