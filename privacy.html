Crab Attack privacy policy

Last updated 7 October 2026

Crab Attack is a beach game you play on your own device. It does not collect, store or share any personal information.

What the game keeps

To remember your progress, the game saves four settings on your device only:

  • your best score
  • the stars you have earned in each round
  • whether sound is muted
  • the towel you picked

These never leave your device. Deleting the app, or clearing the website's data in your browser, removes them.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • No data sent to us or to anyone else. The game makes no network requests while you play.

Website and app

The web version is served by Cloudflare, which handles standard web requests to deliver the page. When you test the iOS app through TestFlight, Apple shares crash reports and any feedback you choose to send with the developer, under Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information, it is suitable for players of any age.

Questions

Ask a question or report a problem on the game's GitHub issues page.
